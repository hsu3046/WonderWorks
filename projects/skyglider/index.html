A NOTE FROM THE FOREST

Trust the wind.

You are a very small glider in a very big world. Leave the old oak and follow the air through the valley. There is no hurry. Before flying, walk along the bough with WASD or arrow keys. After landing, use the same keys to explore the ground; drag to choose your view.
