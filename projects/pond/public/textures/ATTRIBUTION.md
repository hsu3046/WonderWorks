# Garden PBR textures

The following photographic PBR textures are from Poly Haven and are licensed CC0. Each is stored locally at 1024×1024 (diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps). No external texture request is made by the running app.

- Rock Boulder Dry — Dimitrios Savva (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing): https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry
- Bark Brown 02 — Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02
- Forest Ground 04 — Rob Tuytel, Rico Cilliers: https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license

Web modifications: map scale, bark UV orientation, stone triplanar mapping, normal strength, dampness and restrained moss shading. Leaf/grass geometry, veins and wind deformation are generated by the application, not photographed assets.
