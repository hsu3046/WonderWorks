# Skyglider architecture

- `projects/skyglider/src/main.ts`: UI, modal/focus, keyboard shortcuts, scene errors, screenshot download.
- `scene.ts`: one RAF owner, visibility/pause/resize, guided route plus bounded steering, following camera, shared scene time, HDR lighting and post-processing.
- `landscape.ts`: deterministic terrain sampler, route control points and shared bounds. Both geometry and collision guards use the same height function.
- `departure.ts`: branch acquisition, tapered-trunk contact frame, crown mounting, gathering and forward leap. The animal clears the trunk tip before standing on the highest bough, then joins the tour with matching position, velocity and heading.
- `world.ts`: terrain, painted cylindrical mountain panorama, atlas-mapped pines, broadleaf distance LOD, batched architecture and scanned ground details.
- `hero-tree.ts`: curved, tapered oak boughs and twigs, capped contact beams, rounded crown junction and painted foliage clusters.
- `tree-lod.ts`: detailed broadleaf models within 72m, painted crossed-card trees beyond 92m; hysteresis preserves stable membership.
- `prop-lod.ts`: scanned ground details enter within 38m and exit beyond 48m; original transforms restore when returning.
- `world-batches.ts`: procedural suspension bridge merged per material with world transforms preserved. Supplied detailed landmarks share source materials through `local-scenery.ts`.
- `glider.ts`: continuous skinned character, recessed reflective eyes, cupped ears, digits, folding membranes and elliptical tail groom.
- `squirrel-rig.ts`: shared 14-bone rest coordinates for offline authoring and runtime articulation.
- `squirrel-coat.ts`: anatomy-based brown/cream vertex colors and area-weighted, curved, banded fur ribbons. Body strands inherit blended skin weights.
- `squirrel-eyes.ts`: cheek-fitted corneal caps with tapered apertures, dark brown iris pigmentation and upper/lower eyelids; no complete eyeball sphere is placed outside the head.
- `squirrel-whiskers.ts`: independently seeded muzzle follicles fitted to the skin, cubic curves and tapered world-space shafts batched into one mesh on the head bone.
- `squirrel-nose.ts`: a shallow rounded triangular pad fitted to the muzzle, with recessed nostrils, a central cleft and derivative-filtered pores/roughness.
- `squirrel-patagium.ts`: shared wrist/ankle patch, filtered membrane nap and 6,500 dorsal strands per side. The groom follows the same surface on the GPU; only the supporting 48×30 grid is updated on the CPU.
- `scripts/build-squirrel.mjs`: offline implicit sculpt and skin-weight authoring; exports the original `public/assets/squirrel-v3.glb` (10,230 vertices / 20,456 triangles / 576,956 bytes). The runtime loads this local asset in parallel with the environment.
- `character-preview.ts` / `character.html`: close inspection of the same model and materials, with orbit, six camera views (including Top) and four poses (including Running). Static by default; animation and hidden state own a single RAF. Both HTML entries are included in the standalone build.
- `glider-pose.ts`: shared character scale and distance-driven foot cycles. Running pairs forefoot catch and hindfoot push with a suspended recovery, body compression/lift and delayed tail pitch; planted running/climbing feet cancel forward motion.
- `landing.ts`: nearest suitable clearing selection, curved horizontal approach and a sampled, terrain-aware vertical profile with monotone Hermite interpolation. Exact start/end positions prevent a route-switch teleport.
- `water.ts`: painted ocean tile, world-space animated normals, terrain-derived shoreline foam, terrain-fitted illustrated cascades, impact foam and spray.
- `water-layout.ts`: circular sea extent and terrain-derived cascade lip/waterline profiles.
- `terrain-surface.ts`: shared terrain-grid dimensions and Float32-aware interpolation of the actual rendered cliff triangles.
- `meadows.ts`: shared organic meadow mask for ground paint, forest openings and nearby flower sprites.
- `assets.ts`: parallel local asset loading; errors propagate to the visible reload state.

Rendering: WebGL2 → half-float multisampled scene → restrained HDR bloom → OutputPass. ACES tone mapping and sRGB conversion occur once at the final output stage. Device pixel ratio is capped at 1.6. No runtime asset API, backend, authentication or secrets.

The 10.5-second departure approaches the trunk for 2.4 seconds, climbs to 7.65, mounts the crown through 8.5, gathers until 8.95 and leaps forward through 10.5. The tapered trunk ends at 106 scene units; the highest bough supports feet near 107.7. Crown mounting raises the body above the trunk before moving onto that bough and blending into a ground stance. `CLIMB_TRUNK` and `TOP_BRANCH` define both the visible contact geometry and pose coordinates. The actual skinned head remains checked against the trunk. Gathering lowers the center while compensating planted feet; forepaws release, the hind pair extends, legs tuck and the membrane opens.

The push-off position uses a Hermite curve and an arc with zero endpoint vertical derivatives, matching the tour's initial position and velocity. Orientation also reaches the tour's initial heading, replacing the earlier immediate route movement while the character still faced upward. The 82-second glide route uses an arc-length-parameterized Catmull–Rom curve. WASD/arrow steering shifts lateral position and altitude within bounds. Terrain clearance guards protect animal and camera; branches and architecture do not use a general collision solver. Camera orbit yaw/pitch and distance remain user-controlled.

The running start uses `RUN_BRANCH` for both the visible tapered branch and a vertical intersection with its top surface. Before acquiring the trunk, height is that surface plus foot clearance; the gait supplies the short airborne bounds. Acquisition then blends onto the trunk frame while standing the chest upward, so the horizontal snout does not enter the bark. The perch and route endpoint share the branch height. These are contact solutions for the authored departure branch/trunk, not general branch collision.

Flight has four explicit states: `perched → journey → landing → grounded`. `Land nearby` / **L** is enabled after the climb-to-glide handoff; it plans from the actual steered position, clears held input and resumes the scene if paused. The 5–17 second approach selects a nearby clearing below the animal when one is available. Terrain and canopy clearance may require a short rise on the approach. Five small clearings have a level center shared by the terrain mesh and foot placement; trees, rocks, ferns and buildings are excluded near touchdown. Landing unfolds into a stable ground pose rather than resetting to the departure branch. **R** cancels any journey/landing and resets all flight state. Grounded primary action returns to the oak. Walking is available on the departure bough and dry ground after landing; taking off from arbitrary ground remains outside this study.

Climbing poses upper/lower/paw bones within one closed skin, so neck, shoulders and hips remain continuous. Patagium vertices follow the wrists and ankles; the membranes disappear inside the body when folded. Torso shortening, raised shoulders, head angle and tail bend blend out during takeoff. Eye domes sit partly inside the cheek with fine upper eyelids and HDR reflections. The supplied squirrel references informed the rounded haunches, smaller cupped ears, tapered muzzle, pale underside and grizzled brown coat; the former dark dorsal stripe is now subtle shading. The following camera rotates toward the back during the climb and blends back to its flight heading.

The 38,000 body strands plus 10,000 additional facial strands share the skin's skeleton and bind matrix in a single merged groom. Additional facial follicles fade into the neck without perturbing the original body distribution. The 16,000 tail strands and 1,440 ear strands follow their rigid parts. Fur uses outward surface normals on both ribbon faces: Three.js's ordinary double-sided normal flip produced black bands when viewing against the groom, so only the fur material overrides that flip. The skin, membranes and ear surfaces retain normal lighting. Fur geometry and weight assignment are prepared once, rather than rewritten each frame.

The coat has slightly longer, curved strands with thinner body ribbons, reduced tip contrast and broader sheen. The patagium adds 13,000 short dorsal strands over a body-colored nap; its underside stays separate. Both the membrane grain and the groom detail are filtered at small projected sizes. Follicle offsets stay above the supporting grid, preventing upper hairs from appearing through the underside.

Facial nap is shorter than the body coat, with visible banding and lift on the cheek/forehead, shorter muzzle hairs and a smooth blend into the neck groom. Hairs taper away at the eye apertures. Rest-space microtexture and a subtle bump are filtered by screen derivatives. Below their projected footprint, facial strands continuously collapse to their roots in the vertex shader before normal skeletal deformation, leaving the filtered base coat visible. The physical render-target viewport, projection and model scale control this transition, so zoom and DPR are both accounted for. There is no frame-time geometry rebuild, alpha dithering or extra RAF. Whiskers are separate tapered cubic curves with irregular roots, lengths, sweeps, curls and tones; the two sides use independent seeds. Their physical thickness shrinks with distance instead of retaining a fixed screen-space line width.

Explicit pause freezes time and ends RAF after its final invalidated frame. Resize, light changes and camera input can request a single paused render. Hidden tabs and the field-guide modal suspend RAF without overriding the user's pause preference. Resume resets the wall-clock baseline. Page exit disposes owned GPU resources; WebGL context loss shows a reload message.

The worktree reuses committed source `e5cc2df` because the main checkout contains unrelated uncommitted pond GPU-preparation repairs. It uses its own APFS-cloned dependency directory. Branch: `codex/skyglider-valley`; local port: **4182**. Production gallery/build scripts are unchanged.

The painted horizon uses one 128-triangle cylindrical strip with three horizontal texture repeats to preserve the source aspect ratio. Distant broadleaf trees use four triangles each; nearby trees retain all scanned geometry/materials. LOD checks run inside the existing scene RAF only after camera movement of at least 2m. Instance buffers and bounds update only when membership changes. Hysteresis prevents boundary flicker. Terrain and lighting remain 3D; illustration assets and full generation prompts are recorded in `ILLUSTRATION_ASSETS.md`.

Water uses a 2,400m-radius circular surface (160 triangles) with a radial alpha fade beginning at 1,850m, combined with the existing distance fog. It replaces the exposed 1,800m square. The generated water tile supplies color in world coordinates while simple analytical normal waves preserve real environment lighting; no runtime reflection framebuffer or fluid simulation was added. A 201×214 byte shoreline height lookup is prepared once from the shared terrain sampler and disposed with the water system.

Each cascade derives its lip from the front terrain gradient and its impact beyond the local waterline. The 24×96 ribbon samples the actual rendered terrain triangles with 1.1m vertex clearance; regression checks evaluate its triangle interiors against the visible cliff. Analytic terrain noise alone can differ from the coarse mesh and previously let rock cut through the water. The painted veil's UV silhouette and opacity remain fixed; only subtle derivative-filtered light moves downward. Circular foam and depth-tested spray share scene time. Every UniformsUtils-cloned animated layer is reconnected to that clock, so pause and hidden state freeze all layers consistently.

The unified alpine coast panorama paints mountains, forested foothills, coves and islands together. It replaces the v11 coastal strip, whose top-down canopy texture stretched on the slope and whose separate ridge cut across the mountain artwork. One128-triangle cylinder uses three horizontal repeats, a1.22 vertical repeat and a-.02 vertical offset. Its low sea band fades over geometry UV .10–.13; the 3D sea draws first (renderOrder-2), the painted backdrop next(-1), with ordinary depth testing. This lets real water obscure underwater art while preserving painted headlands in front of more distant water. New313,954-byte panorama replaces both previous runtime coast textures; old files remain as provenance. No new RAF or dynamic geometry upload. Asset provenance: `ILLUSTRATION_ASSETS_V12.md`.

During bark acquisition the tail now curls outward/upward until it clears the departure bough, then gradually hangs below the body again as height increases. CLIMB_START_HEIGHT81.4 places hips and the tail root above the bough; departurePosition supplies the continuous tailLift weight. The scene passes it explicitly to the shared glider, while standalone previews retain their normal tail pose. Tail length/volume are unchanged. Regression checks evaluate actual tail skin vertices and every guard-hair tip against the rendered ground and authored trunk/bough volumes over92 departure poses and three idle-clock phases. Solid tail stays outside the wood; soft tips may brush it by up to15cm.

Seven organic meadow regions blend the generated top-down flower texture into gently sloping terrain. The same spatial mask preserves five landing pads and opens small areas in the pine/broadleaf forest. Nearby daisies, buttercups and lilac bells use two crossed painted cards per tuft, with gentle shader sway. Flower LOD enters at65m and exits at85m; the ground painting remains at any viewing distance. All work stays inside the existing scene RAF. Water and meadow texture provenance/full prompts: `ILLUSTRATION_ASSETS_V10.md`.

- `local-scenery-layout.ts`: supplied asset positions and reserved footprints; landing clearings remain open.
- `local-scenery.ts`: normalized local models, terrain-fitted architecture, instanced flowers with 45/58m LOD.
- `woodland-rill.ts`: terrain-grid-aligned shallow stream below the supplied arched bridge.
- `scripts/prepare-local-scenery.mjs`: offline reductions, compacted vertices and 1K WebP embedded maps; provenance/checksums in `public/assets/local/processing.json`.
- `asset-review.html` / `asset-review.ts`: dev-only one-model inspection, orbit and resource cleanup. Main and character production entries are unchanged. See `LOCAL_ASSETS.md`.


Building scales are based on the precise transformed GLB extents in `scenery-scale.ts`. All seven houses use height20, the castle60 and both gazebos7.2; source physical units are unspecified. Layout reservations derive from scaled width/depth half-diagonals. Rigid placement checks actual bottom mesh vertices after yaw against `renderedTerrainHeight`, then fits its foundation; flower instances exclude those same expanded reservations. `scripts/audit-scenery-scale.mjs` independently measures the processed GLBs and reports source extents, scale factors and actual world bounds to `docs/skyglider/SCENERY_SCALE_AUDIT.json`.


Walking uses `walking.ts` in the existing scene RAF. WASD/arrows move relative to the ground camera, with normalized diagonals, eased acceleration, movement-distance foot phases and a view direction that stays fixed while the animal turns. On the departure bough, W/A move toward the trunk and S/D toward the tip; the supported path is bounded to six units. Departure snapshots that walked position and facing, then joins the authored climbing/tour handoff continuously.

Ground movement samples the actual rendered terrain, rejects water, steep slopes and world edges, and substeps against static landmark/tree/rock collision proxies. Proxies share the visible placement filters and remain stable across LOD. Gentle slopes orient the body; each stance paw separately samples the ground, adjusts upper/lower/paw pose, and preserves its recovery lift. This avoids raising the whole animal to the highest distant foot probe, which floats feet above curved ground. The membrane stays folded; walking bypasses the paired bounding motion used by the scripted running start. Ground takeoff is still via return to the oak.

Pause, modal, blur and hidden state clear walk input; paused frames cannot move or advance stride. Mobile directional controls use the same input owner as flight and switch their labels by mode. No new RAF, physics dependency, fur rebuild or geometry upload is added. Tests cover five clearings, diagonal speed, pause, cliff/prop stops, branch bounds, walked departure continuity and actual paw contact over the visible terrain.
